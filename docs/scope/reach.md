What lets buyers find AfriDeal, read it in their language, and trust it: legal pages, SEO, Setswana, and a real catalogue with real photographs. Back to [index.md](index.md).

## Slice 6: Run it safely

### 17. Legal pages & cookie notice · Beta
Terms of sale, a privacy policy under the Data Protection Act 2018, and a returns policy, linked from the footer and checkout, plus a short cookie notice. The copy comes from the client.
**Done when:** the three pages are live on staging with client approved copy; checkout links to terms and returns; the cookie notice shows on first visit without blocking the page.
