## AfriDeal privacy notice

Last updated: [date]

This notice explains what personal information AfriDeal collects, why, where
we keep it, how long we keep it, and what you can do about it. It applies to
everyone who uses AfriDeal: buyers, suppliers, runners and visitors, in
Botswana, South Africa, Lesotho and Eswatini.

### Who we are

AfriDeal is run by [company name], a company registered in Botswana
(registration number [number]), at [physical address]. We decide how your
information is used, so the law calls us the data controller (in South Africa,
the "responsible party").

Our data protection officer (in South Africa, our Information Officer) is
[name or title]. Contact them at **[privacy@afrideal.co.bw]**, [phone], or by
post at [address]. You can write in English, Setswana or Sesotho.

### The laws that protect you

- Botswana: Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 18 of 2024)
- South Africa: Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA)
- Lesotho: Data Protection Act, 2011 (Act No. 5 of 2012)
- Eswatini: Data Protection Act, 2022 (Act No. 5 of 2022)

You have the rights of the law of the country you live in. Where they differ,
we give everyone the stronger protection.

### What we collect and why

| What | Why | Our legal reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Your account**: name, email address, phone number, password (held by our sign-in provider, never by us), the type of buyer you are | To open and secure your account, sign you in, and show you the right prices | To perform our contract with you |
| **Your orders**: what you bought, quantities, prices, the supplier, order and payment status, refunds, disputes, messages with us | To sell and deliver your order, handle returns and refunds, and keep the records the law requires | Contract; legal duty (tax and accounting records) |
| **Delivery details**: delivery address, town and country, collection point, the name and phone number of the person receiving | To deliver, or to let a runner or courier contact you | Contract |
| **Payments**: card payments are taken by Stripe. We see the payment reference, amount, status and the last four digits and brand of the card, never the full card number | To take payment, refund, and prevent fraud | Contract; legal duty |
| **Quote and runner requests**: what you asked for, where, and the replies | To answer your request | Contract, or steps you asked for before a contract |
| **Runner applications** (only if you apply to drive): name, phone, vehicle and plate, the towns you work in, and copies of your identity document (Omang, national ID or passport), driver's licence and vehicle registration | To check who you are and that you may drive before you deliver for us | Our legitimate interest in safe deliveries, and steps you asked for before a contract |
| **Suppliers' contact people**: name, work email and phone, company registration details | To run the supplier account and pay the supplier | Contract |
| **Technical information**: sign-in records, the device and browser used, and security logs | To keep accounts and the site secure and fix faults | Our legitimate interest in a secure service |
| **Marketing choices**: whether you agreed to offers by email, SMS or WhatsApp, and when | To send offers only to people who said yes, and to prove it | Your consent |

You must give the account, order and delivery details to buy; without them we
cannot sell or deliver. Everything marked as marketing is optional.

We do not sell personal information. We do not make decisions about you by
automated means alone that have legal or similarly significant effects. Prices
depend on the buyer type and quantity you choose, not on a profile of you.

AfriDeal is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect
information about children.

### Where we keep it, and sending it abroad

Our systems are hosted in the **United States of America**. We also keep a
copy of your information **in Botswana**, as Botswana's law requires.

The USA does not have a data protection law equivalent to ours. We protect
your information abroad by:

- signing data processing agreements with each provider that bind them to
  keep it confidential and secure, use it only for us, and pass it on only
  under equally strong terms;
- encrypting it in transit and at rest; and
- asking for your agreement to hosting abroad when you create an account.

Without hosting abroad we cannot run your account, so if you do not agree we
cannot open one. You may ask us for a copy of the safeguards we use.

### How long we keep it

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Account | While your account is open. If you close it, we delete it within 30 days, except what we must keep below |
| Orders, payments and refunds | [7] years after the order, because tax and accounting laws require it. After you close your account we keep these with your name removed where we can |
| Unpaid or cancelled orders that never took payment | 12 months |
| Delivery details | With the order they belong to |
| Runner application documents | 90 days after a rejection or withdrawal; while you are an active runner and for 1 year after you stop |
| Security logs | [90 days] |
| Marketing consent records | While you are subscribed, and 3 years after you unsubscribe, to prove what you agreed to |
| Messages and in-app notifications | 12 months |

When the period ends we delete the information or remove anything that
identifies you, in a way that cannot be undone.

### Your rights

You can ask us, free of charge, to:

- **tell you** whether we hold information about you, and give you a copy
  (we can send it as a file you can take elsewhere);
- **correct** anything wrong or incomplete;
- **delete** information we no longer need or should not have;
- **restrict** how we use it while a question is being settled;
- **stop** using it for marketing, at any time, and object to other uses based
  on our legitimate interests; and
- **withdraw your consent** at any time. This does not affect what we did
  before.

Signed in, you can download your information and ask for your account to be
closed from [Account → Privacy]. Or write to our data protection officer. We
may ask you to prove who you are. We will reply within **14 days**, and if we
cannot do what you ask we will tell you why.

### Marketing

We send offers by email, SMS or WhatsApp **only if you said yes**. Every
offer says it is from AfriDeal and tells you how to stop. Messages about your
own orders (order placed, paid, out for delivery) are not marketing and are
sent to every buyer.

### Security, and if something goes wrong

We limit who can see your information, encrypt it, keep identity documents in
private storage reachable only through links that expire in minutes, and log
staff access. If your information is exposed in a way that puts you at risk,
we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires, explain what happened
and what you can do.

### Cookies

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in and secure. Your cart is
saved on your own device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
